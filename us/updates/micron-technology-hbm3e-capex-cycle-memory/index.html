1. Forensic Thesis & Strategic Deconstruction

The transition from standard server DRAM to 12-layer HBM3E stacks requires 3x more wafer capacity per bit. Because Micron's entire HBM production is pre-sold under contract through 2026, the company enjoys structural pricing power and counter-cyclical margin protection.

Capital allocation decisions over a multi-year horizon reveal structural operating leverage. Examining statutory footnotes and segment reporting isolates core operating profitability from discretionary adjustments and accounting revisions.

2. Financial Verification & Cash Flow Conversion

FY26 revenue rose 37.2% YoY to $34.5B with DRAM revenue contributing $24.8B (72% of total). Gross margin expanded to 41.5% with operating cash flow reaching $11.2B against capital expenditures of $14.1B, maintaining a net cash position of $2.4B.

A granular reconciliation of operating cash flows against reported earnings confirms whether revenue expansion is generating unencumbered free cash flow. Working capital cycles, inventory velocity, and trade receivable aging provide transparent evidence of underlying pricing power.
